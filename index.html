01 / THE INSIGHT

Equal error. Unequal consequences.

A quantization error’s effect depends on the stream, layer, and denoising step. SteerQuant uses final-action sensitivity to guide calibration.

02 / THE METHOD

Steer the error.
Keep the weights shared.

01

Map action impact

02

Route shared-weight error

Calibrate channel scaling using the impact map.

03

Modulate each stream
